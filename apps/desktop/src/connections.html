Connections

Recast places things only in accounts you connect here, with your own permissions. Everything stays on this computer: the app keeps the keys and tokens, readable only by your user.

Google (Calendar, Gmail drafts, Tasks, Docs)

  1. In Google Cloud, create an OAuth client of type Desktop app or Web application with the redirect address http://localhost:8787/oauth/callback, and add yourself under Test users.
  2. Paste its Client ID and Client secret here.
  3. Then sign in with Google in your browser and tick what Recast may do.

Outlook Calendar (Microsoft 365)

  1. In portal.azure.com → App registrations → New registration: platform Mobile and desktop applications with the redirect address http://localhost:8787/microsoft/callback; API permission Microsoft Graph → Delegated → Calendars.ReadWrite. No client secret is needed.
  2. Paste its Application (client) ID here.
  3. Then sign in with Microsoft in your browser.

GitHub, Linear and Todoist

Connect a GitHub repository, a Linear team or your Todoist on the sign-in page (it opens in your browser), each with its own token.

Maps

Google Maps (and Apple Maps on a Mac) need nothing: a place opens directions in your browser or Maps app.
