Anywhere: tap ⌥ Option twice, then select text. It becomes a blob right there; click it to choose where it goes.

Allow Recast to see the trigger key and what you select. macOS asks once: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on Recast. Then quit Recast and open it again.

Allow Recast to see the screen, for highlights. When Recast is on, it reads what is on the screen (here, on this Mac; nothing is sent or kept) to highlight times, email addresses and errors you can click. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording → turn on Recast. Selecting by hand works without it.

  1. In any app (a browser, a chat, a document), tap ⌥ Option twice. Recast is on, right there; nothing opens.
  2. Select some text: a time, an email address, an error, a sentence. It lifts into a blob beside it.
  3. Drag one blob onto another to press them together. Click a blob and the wheel opens beside it: choose where it goes, review, place.
  4. Press Esc (or the trigger key once) to turn Recast off. Everything you grabbed is kept here.
